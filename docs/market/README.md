# Market artifacts

The `$maximize-market-learning` brain owns these stores. `$market-recon` appends
evidence to them; nothing here is rewritten to fit a later story.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `niche-map.md` | Under-served intersections, their exploitation thesis, and the dry-up signal being watched |
| `failure-ledger.md` | Dead or abandoned predecessors, cited cause of death, cause class |
| `paygate-teardowns.md` | Per-competitor: free vs gated, value metric, price points, gaps left open |

Persona-scoped findings (alternatives sets, watering holes, mistaken-identity
sets) live in `personas/` at the repo root, not here — they are product-agnostic
cohort properties.

**Every claim in these files carries a source, a date, and a confidence.**
Observation and inference are kept visibly separate. Read
`recon-2026-08-09.md` for the upward contract of the run that populated them,
including what external research structurally cannot see.
